Every benefit you qualify for. One place to find it.

Welfare schemes, scholarships and farm subsidies are scattered across dozens of departmental portals. JanSetu brings them into a single searchable knowledge base, tells you in plain language what you qualify for, and gives you a direct path to escalate when your money or approval is stuck.
